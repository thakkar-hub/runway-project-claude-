# EPISODE 01 — "8:47" · Pre-production breakdown

## 1. Episode definition

1. **Continuation point:** Series open. We enter mid-vibration with no establishing shot.
2. **Objective:** Sasha needs to get out the door looking like someone who has it together.
3. **New information:** She's late for something she can't skip, and someone else knows what it is.
4. **Emotional shift:** Groggy → panic → forced composure → unease.
5. **Cliffhanger:** *Whatever happens, don't tell them.* Who are "them," and what is she hiding?
6. **Final state:** Bathroom. She holds the phone at chest height with the message open, and her reflection stares at the screen. The tap is still running.
7. **Wardrobe/continuity:**
   - **Wakes in:** a faded grey oversized tee, with hair in a collapsing claw-clip bun.
   - **By shot 6:** a crumpled white shirt with the top two buttons undone and one cuff unbuttoned. Hair half-tied with flyaways.
   - **Makeup:** none, except one swipe of kajal under her **left eye only**. It's already there when she wakes; it's yesterday's, and she never notices it.
   - **Jewellery:** small gold studs throughout.
8. **Props:** Phone with a cracked corner screen protector (same corner in every shot). Steel tumbler with toothbrushes. The mirror cabinet is slightly desilvered at the edges. A clothes pile on a chair.
9. **Sound:** Phone vibrating on wood, ceiling fan, a distant Mumbai horn, the tap running, toothbrush scrub, and a low pulse underscore that drops out on the text.
10. **Screen inserts:** The "8:47" lock screen, the caller ID, and the message. All three are composited in post, never generated text.

**Caller ID:** "K.". It keeps the mystery and reads cleanly on screen. Swap it if the season arc already has a name.

### SHOT 01 — 2.5 sec (0.0–2.5)
- **Framing:** ECU of the phone vibrating on a wooden bedside table in the foreground. Sasha is soft in the background, face half in the pillow.
- **Action:** The phone skitters slightly. She jolts awake and her hand slaps toward it. Rack focus to her.
- **Performance:** Puffy-eyed and disoriented. She doesn't snap awake cleanly.
- **Camera:** Locked off, with a slow focus pull only.
- **Sound:** Harsh vibration rattling the wood, fan hum, a horn far away.
- **Continuity:** Morning light comes through window grilles in bars across the sheet.

### SHOT 02 — 1.5 sec (2.5–4.0)
- **Framing:** OTS from behind her shoulder onto the lock screen, which reads **8:47 AM**, large and legible (composite).
- **Dialogue:** SASHA (low, flat): "Shit."
- **Performance:** We barely see her face. The word does the work.
- **Sound:** The vibration cuts out as she silences it, leaving dead quiet for half a beat.

### SHOT 03 — 1.5 sec (4.0–5.5)
- **Framing:** Static wide of a small, lived-in bedroom with clothes on a chair and a half-open cupboard.
- **Action:** She launches out of bed. Her foot catches the sheet and she stumbles but doesn't fall, then exits frame.
- **Camera:** Locked off. The comedy comes from the stillness.
- **Sound:** Bare feet slapping tile, the sheet dragging, a cupboard door banging (off).

### SHOT 04 — 3.5 sec (5.5–9.0)
- **Framing:** Medium on her mirror reflection in the bathroom, with the same framing held across 4 jump cuts:
  - (a) She brushes aggressively, the tap running.
  - (b) She has the white shirt half on, one arm in, and checks the collar.
  - (c) She yanks her hair into a tie, misses some, gives up.
  - (d) She checks her breath into her cupped palm, then tries a "professional" smile at the mirror, and the smile dies instantly.
- **Camera:** Slight handheld, as if someone is standing in the doorway.
- **Sound:** Scrubbing, water, fabric snap, and a hard edit on each jump with no whooshes.
- **Continuity:** The tap is turned on in (a) and is **never turned off**.

### SHOT 05 — 0.8 sec (9.0–9.8)
- **Framing:** Insert of the phone ringing on the sink ledge, screen showing **K.** (composite). Her hand freezes mid-reach at the frame edge.
- **Sound:** Ringtone over the running tap. The CALLER pre-laps: "We're on for—"

### SHOT 06 — 3.2 sec (9.8–13.0)
- **Framing:** Over her shoulder into the mirror. We see her reflection with the phone to her ear and her real shoulder soft in the foreground.
- **Dialogue:** CALLER (V.O., calm, unhurried, continuing the pre-lap): "—today?"
  SASHA (eyes on her own reflection, distracted): "Yeah… I don't really have an option."
  Then a CLICK, and the line is dead.
- **Performance:** She swallows before answering and avoids her own eyes in the mirror. After the click she finally looks at herself and holds it a beat too long.
- **Sound:** The caller is phone-filtered. The tap is still running and she doesn't notice.

### SHOT 07 — FINAL — 2.0 sec (13.0–15.0)
- **Framing:** Close OTS onto the phone in her hand at chest height. Her reflection sits soft behind it in the mirror.
- **Action:** The phone buzzes and a message slides in: **Whatever happens, don't tell them.** (composite, legible, nothing else on screen).
- **Performance:** Her reflection goes still. There's no big reaction, just a slight swallow.
- **Sound:** The music drops out entirely. Only the tap runs.
- **Cliffhanger:** The message.
- **Exact cut point:** Hard cut on the swallow, before she types or looks up.
- **State to preserve for Ep 02:** Bathroom, tap running, message open, shirt half-buttoned, hair half-tied, kajal on the left eye only, gold studs, phone at chest height (cracked corner the same), morning light.

## 3. Post notes
- Composite all three screen texts (8:47 AM / K. / the message) in a simple system font. Track the phone's screen plane. Never use generated text.
- Subtitles, if used, should be small, off-white, low in the safe area, with a subtle shadow.
- An optional opening card can read **EPISODE 01 — 8:47**.
- Keep the Social Salsaa branding to the end frame only, or leave it out.
- Grade for late-90s film: gentle grain, soft highlight bloom, muted blacks, warm skin. No teal-orange.

## 4. QC checklist (reject and regenerate if any fail)
- Sasha's face is the same across all 7 shots: eye shape, hairline, nose, jaw.
- Kajal is under the **left** eye only (watch for mirror flips in shots 04, 06 and 07: in the reflection it appears on screen-right).
- The phone shows no warped geometry, and the cracked corner is the same corner in every shot.
- No extra fingers on her hand reaching for or holding the phone (01, 05, 07).
- The tap is visibly running in 04, 05, 06 and 07.
- There's no slow motion, floating camera or waxy skin.
